Prospective after the immutable stopped P39N family,8f63488. No old operation
is resumed, replaced or pooled. No source candidate, quiet timing, allocation

Question: can the exact original version25 p39n2/p39n3 normal modules produce
complete optimized FromEncoded callers after explicitly bounded additional
invocation priming? Original500ms native diagnostics lack those callers and
the candidate's FromEncoded case has no retained optimized listing. The stopped
normal family shows slower FromEncoded despite faster standalone validation;
tier history and persistent generated-code cost remain competing explanations.

API. Stage its identical bytes/deps/runtime into two task-owned directories,
with exact original normal DLL/dependency bytes. Each process loads one actual
library only. Original CompactNormal binaries are never rebuilt or edited.

disabled, compiler11.0.100-rc.2.26504.105. Diagnostic JitDisasm observes original
CompactNormal methods; this footprint is explicitly distinct from quiet timing.

diagnostic family. No retries, sample expansion or reruns until a preferred tier

the duration-controlled counts as comparable throughput observations.
